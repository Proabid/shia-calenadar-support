Privacy policy — Shia Calenadar

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Who is responsible

Abdulbari abdlbari develops Shia Calenadar. Contact: ettavett@gmail.com.

Data in the app

The app does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking tools or third-party SDKs. Calendar data is bundled with the app and works offline.

Your language, calendar visibility and reminder preferences are stored on your device. iOS schedules local notifications with your permission. No remote push notification service is used. Apple-managed backups may retain app settings according to your device settings.

External websites

When you open an original source link, your browser visits sistani.org. That website's privacy practices apply. This support site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard web request information, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. We add no analytics or tracking scripts.

Support emails

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request, not for advertising. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve and follow up the request or meet applicable obligations. You may contact us to request deletion. Email is processed by the email provider.

Your choices

You can change or disable reminders in the app, revoke notification permission in device Settings, or delete the app to remove its local preferences. Existing backups are managed through Apple's device and account settings.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy declarations will be updated.
